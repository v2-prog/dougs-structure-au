Room 4 — Superannuation Basics

Brief explanation

Superannuation is long-term savings for retirement. Employers must pay a percentage of your ordinary earnings into a super fund (the Superannuation Guarantee). You can choose your fund, consolidate accounts, and check investment options. Starting early and keeping fees low can make a large difference over decades because of compounding.
