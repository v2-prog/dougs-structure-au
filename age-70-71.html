Five economic considerations

Minimum drawdown

Super pensions must pay a minimum amount each year.

Maintenance costs

Driving less

Regular medication and health visits are a fixed cost.

Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

A fixed income calls for careful budgeting. Concessions on health, transport and energy help.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Steady income covers regular spending. Home maintenance and health costs are the main variables.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Spare capital goes to gifts, travel and estate planning. Advice is worthwhile for large estates.
