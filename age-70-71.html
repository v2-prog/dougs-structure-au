Five economic considerations

Minimum drawdown

Super pensions must pay a minimum amount each year.

SourceAustralian Taxation Office

Maintenance costs

Home upkeep costs rise, and some people downsize.

SourceHousing Australia

Driving less

Fewer trips, and licence checks start to apply in some states.

Sourceaustralia.gov.au

Medication

Regular medication and health visits are a fixed cost.

SourceDepartment of Health

Estate planning

Wills and enduring powers of attorney need to be current.

SourceMoneySmart (ASIC)

Three income scenarios

Income ranges for people aged 70 to 74 who lodged a 2022–23 tax return. Lower is the bottom 10%, middle is the 40th to 60th percentile and higher is the top 10%. Figures are taxable income a year, in 2022–23 dollars, and show where you sit in this age group. Tax-free super payments from age 60 are not counted in taxable income, so real spending power can be higher than the figures shown.

Bottom 10%

Lower income

Up to about $3,500 a year

A fixed income calls for careful budgeting. Concessions on health, transport and energy help.

If this is you

  • Use Services Australia's Financial Information Service for free guidance.
  • Check Pensioner Concession Card benefits.

Professionals to consider: Services Australia Financial Information Service officer, financial counsellor, a seniors legal or rights service in your state.

40th to 60th percentile

Middle income

$22,000 to $35,500 a year

Income is steady from the pension or super. Rising costs mean checking bills and cover each year.

If this is you

  • Review private health cover against costs.
  • Plan home maintenance and future needs early.

Professionals to consider: Licensed financial adviser (retirement income), registered tax agent, estate planning lawyer, My Aged Care assessor for future care.

Top 10%

Higher income

Above about $105,000 a year

Higher retirement income from investments and super. Minimum drawdown rules, tax and estate planning are the focus.

If this is you

  • Check the minimum annual pension payment from super.
  • Talk to an adviser about gifting, family support and estate planning.

Professionals to consider: Licensed financial adviser (retirement and aged care), accountant, elder law or estate planning lawyer.

Source: ATO Taxation statistics 2022–23, Individuals Table 3 (all individuals who lodged a return, men and women combined). Percentiles are interpolated within the ATO income ranges. Taxable income is after deductions and before tax. People who did not lodge a return are not counted. Examples only, not advice. Check advisers on ASIC's Financial Advisers Register and tax agents on the Tax Practitioners Board register.
