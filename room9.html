Room 9 — Cooperatives

Brief explanation

A cooperative is a business owned and controlled by its members who use its services. Members share decision-making and benefits. Australia has a long history of co-ops in agriculture, housing, finance and retail. They can be a practical way for groups to achieve something together that would be harder alone, with democratic governance and often a focus on community benefit.
