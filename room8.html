Room 8 — Trusts & Family Structures

Brief explanation

Australian structures such as discretionary trusts, companies or SMSFs can hold assets for families or purposes. They have different tax, control and succession rules. A charity or community land trust with an asset lock is not a substitute for a family discretionary trust. These tools are for specific situations and usually require professional setup and ongoing compliance.
