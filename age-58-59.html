Three income scenarios

Income ranges for people aged 55 to 59 who lodged a 2022–23 tax return. Lower is the bottom 10%, middle is the 40th to 60th percentile and higher is the top 10%. Figures are taxable income a year, in 2022–23 dollars, and show where you sit in this age group.

Bottom 10%

Lower income

Up to about $16,500 a year

Super and any Age Pension entitlement shape the retirement date. Working a little longer can make a large difference to a small balance.

If this is you

  • Understand how the Age Pension income and assets tests treat super and savings.
  • Check what insurance your super fund provides and when it ends.

Professionals to consider: Services Australia Financial Information Service officer (Age Pension questions), financial counsellor, community legal centre.

40th to 60th percentile

Middle income

$55,500 to $81,000 a year

Planning for the last working years. Decisions about super, the home and any downsizing are close.

If this is you

  • Consider downsizer contributions into super, available from age 55 if eligible.
  • Talk to an adviser about drawdown order and tax.

Professionals to consider: Licensed financial adviser with retirement income experience, registered tax agent, estate planning lawyer.

Top 10%

Higher income

Above about $169,000 a year

Top-tenth income at this age means retirement choices are mostly about tax, structure and legacy. Timing of super withdrawals shapes tax paid.

If this is you

  • Model transition to retirement and full retirement scenarios.
  • Review estate planning, including testamentary trusts.

Professionals to consider: Licensed financial adviser (CFP, retirement income), accountant, SMSF specialist, estate planning lawyer.

Source: ATO Taxation statistics 2022–23, Individuals Table 3 (all individuals who lodged a return, men and women combined). Percentiles are interpolated within the ATO income ranges. Taxable income is after deductions and before tax. People who did not lodge a return are not counted. Examples only, not advice. Check advisers on ASIC's Financial Advisers Register and tax agents on the Tax Practitioners Board register.
