Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Super and any Age Pension entitlement shape retirement date. Working a little longer can make a large difference.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Retirement date options become real, balancing super, mortgage and lifestyle.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Retirement timing is flexible. Tax in retirement and estate planning are central.
