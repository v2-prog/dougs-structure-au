Room 6 — Housing, Renting & Buying

Brief explanation

Renting gives flexibility but involves bond, inspections and rights under tenancy laws (which differ by state/territory). Buying a home usually requires a deposit, stamp duty, ongoing costs and a long-term commitment. Government schemes and grants exist for eligible first-home buyers, but rules change. Understand the full cost of either path before deciding.
