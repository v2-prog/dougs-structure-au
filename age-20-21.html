Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Rent in a share house takes a big slice of pay. A used car may be financed with a high-interest loan, which is worth comparing carefully.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Rent, a car and some savings are all manageable. A first holiday or tools for work can be bought without borrowing.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Early investing, a newer car and a rental in a preferred suburb are all realistic. Super and shares start to compound.
