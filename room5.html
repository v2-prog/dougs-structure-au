Room 5 — Investing Fundamentals

Brief explanation

Investing means putting money into assets (such as shares, ETFs or property) with the aim of growing it over time. It carries risk — values can fall as well as rise. Diversification (spreading money across different investments) and a long time horizon can help manage risk. Only invest money you can afford to leave for the longer term after you have an emergency buffer and high-interest debt under control.
