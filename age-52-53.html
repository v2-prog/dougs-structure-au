Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Focus on clearing debts and any available catch-up contributions. Even small amounts help over eight to ten years.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

A good window for extra super and mortgage repayments before work reduces.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Maximising concessional contributions and planning tax-effective investments are typical.
