Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Rising bills and rates may force budget cuts. Hardship support from lenders and utilities is available.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Budgets are tight but stable. Comparing loan rates and energy plans can save thousands a year.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Surplus cashflow goes to extra repayments, school fees and investing. Tax planning becomes more worthwhile.
