Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Government support covers most care costs. Understanding fees and rights is important.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Accommodation payments may be funded by selling the home. Advice helps with the timing.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Accommodation and care are funded privately. Estate and legacy planning are central.
