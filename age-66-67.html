Five economic considerations

Age Pension age

The Age Pension age is 67. Claims can usually be lodged shortly before eligibility.

SourceServices Australia

Medicare costs

Medicare covers many costs but out-of-pocket expenses grow.

SourceDepartment of Health

Home equity

The family home is exempt from the pension assets test, but it is a key asset.

SourceServices Australia

Assets and income tests

Pension is reduced as income and assets rise above set limits.

Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Age Pension can be the main income. Rent assistance may apply to renters.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

A part-pension may be paid depending on assets and income. Super drawdowns and pension combine.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Income and assets are often too high for the pension. Private income, tax and estate planning matter more.
