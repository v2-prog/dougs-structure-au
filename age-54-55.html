Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Costs fall as the family leaves home, so extra repayments or super are possible.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Strong saving years. Retirement date and lifestyle begin to take shape.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Wealth management and estate planning take more attention.
