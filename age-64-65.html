64–65

Ocean Depths row · ages 62 to 71

Five economic considerations

Average retirement age

The average retirement age is around 64 to 65.

SourceAustralian Bureau of Statistics

Spending shift

Spending moves from work-related costs to leisure and health.

SourceMoneySmart (ASIC)

Concession cards

Seniors cards and concessions can reduce bills and transport costs.

SourceServices Australia

Downsizing

Many downsize to release equity and reduce upkeep.

SourceHousing Australia

Pension vs lump sum

Super income stream decisions affect later pension entitlements.

SourceAustralian Taxation Office

Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Concession cards and rebates help. The Age Pension eligibility date is a key planning point.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Super income and savings fund retirement. Concessions reduce costs.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Complex income sources, tax and estate planning remain the focus.
