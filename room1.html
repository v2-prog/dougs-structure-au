Room 1 — Money Basics & Budgeting

Brief explanation

A budget is simply a plan for your money. It shows what comes in (income) and what goes out (expenses). Knowing the difference between needs and wants helps you make choices that leave room for saving or goals. Start with a simple snapshot of the last fortnight or month using bank statements or a banking app.
