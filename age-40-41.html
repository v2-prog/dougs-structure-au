Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Career change may be needed if work is physically demanding or insecure. Reskilling support may be available.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

A solid year for saving. Choosing between mortgage repayments and investing is a common decision.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Strong income allows both an offset account and portfolio growth. Retirement planning can start early.
