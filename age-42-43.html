Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Teenage costs rise faster than pay. Cost-of-living support and cheaper activities help stretch the budget.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Two incomes keep things steady. The family car and holiday are balanced with saving for retirement.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Private school fees may be the largest single cost, alongside investing and holidays.
