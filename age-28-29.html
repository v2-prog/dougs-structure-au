Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

A single income may stretch to a small purchase only with a scheme or help. Renting while saving remains common.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Two incomes and a scheme-backed deposit may open the door to a first purchase. Budgeting for stamp duty and legal costs is key.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Borrowing power is high, but location and long-term cashflow decide the purchase. Investment property may be considered.
