Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Super is small, and the Age Pension at 67 is important. Budgeting carefully until then matters.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Super covers early retirement. Part-time work may bridge the gap to the Age Pension.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Super pension income is tax-free and planned. Estate and investment strategies lead.
