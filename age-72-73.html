Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Fixed income means rising bills matter most. Concessions and hardship schemes are important.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

A balanced budget with some spare. Health and home costs are the main swing factors.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Capital supports lifestyle, gifting and care choices. Estate planning continues.
