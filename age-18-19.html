Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Youth Allowance and part-time work are likely to be the main income. Living at home often keeps study affordable.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Study or full-time work with a car and a share-house or home arrangement. HELP debt is common for university students.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Family support may cover fees and rent, so HELP debt is smaller or avoided. Savings and investments may start early.
