Economic life stages

What typically changes in a person’s money at each two-year age band, from 12 to 81, in Australia. Open a card for each year’s events, men and women, and rich and poor scenarios.

Figures are general Australian averages (for example: leaving home at 23–24, average HECS debt about $28,000, first home buyers aged 34–37, median age of losing a parent about 50, presbyopia from about 40, average hysterectomy age 54, median entry to permanent aged care 85, life expectancy 85.8 for women and 82.3 for men). Individual outcomes vary widely. This is general information, not financial, legal or medical advice.
