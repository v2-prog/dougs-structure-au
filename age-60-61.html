Five economic considerations

Super preservation age

Preservation age is 60 for people born after 1 July 1964, so super can be accessed on retirement.

SourceAustralian Taxation Office

Many cut back or retire

Some people retire or reduce work now.

SourceMoneySmart (ASIC)

Health spending

Health spending rises, and private cover needs review.

SourceDepartment of Health

Transition to retirement

Pension from super while still working can be used for tax planning.

SourceAustralian Taxation Office

Home equity

The home is often the largest asset.

Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Super may give limited income, so the Age Pension will play a larger role. Staying employed is valuable.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Part-pension is possible depending on assets. A mix of super, savings and part-time work is common.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Super funds a tax-free retirement income. Estate planning and wealth transfer are key.
