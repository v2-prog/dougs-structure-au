Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

One parent may return to work early to keep up with rent or mortgage. Family Tax Benefit and subsidies are important to check.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Leave from work and a temporary income dip are manageable with savings. Childcare plans start early.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Extended leave, private obstetric care and a larger home are all possible. Super top-ups can offset time out of work.
