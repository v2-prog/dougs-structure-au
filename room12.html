Room 12 — Action Planning

Brief explanation

Knowledge becomes useful when you turn it into small, concrete next steps that fit your actual situation. Choose one immediate action (check a payslip, open a separate savings account, look up your super), one learning goal, and one support contact you would use if needed. Progress is personal. Revisit the rooms as your circumstances change. Free independent help is available if you feel stuck or overwhelmed.
