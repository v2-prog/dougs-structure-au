Room 2 — Saving & Emergency Funds

Brief explanation

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected costs such as car repairs, medical bills or a temporary loss of income. Aim for a starter amount (even $500–$1,000) then build toward 1–3 months of essential expenses. Keep it separate from everyday spending so it is there when needed. Automating a small transfer each pay helps it grow without relying on willpower alone.
