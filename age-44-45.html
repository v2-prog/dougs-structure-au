Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

A side business or extra shifts may help, but income can be irregular. Understanding tax and ABN rules protects against surprises.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Good income with teenage costs. Extra super contributions and mortgage repayments compete for spare cash.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Income is rising, and structuring investments tax-effectively becomes a real question.
