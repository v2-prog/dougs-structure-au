# Ages 40–41: Peak Family Costs & Career Growth

**Educational only. Not personal advice. Verify current rules.**

## Life Events
- Children in school; sport, activity and education costs rise.
- Senior roles or management possible.
- Mortgage often at its heaviest.
- Super contributions can be maximised with higher income.

## Scenarios
### Scenario A: Family of five
High living costs. Dual income important. Extra mortgage repayments if possible.

### Scenario B: Property portfolio + SMSF preparation
Multiple properties. Debt recycling and future SMSF transition planning.

### Scenario C: Eco-village exploration
Investigating land, CLT or cooperative models while children are still at home (Structure Lab).

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Large family home.
- Multi-generational household.
- Regional acreage.
- Cooperative or community living models.

## Wealth Level Snapshot (couple)
| Level | Combined Super | Other Assets | Age Pension Path |
|-------|----------------|--------------|------------------|
| Modest | $250–450k | Building equity | Partial later |
| Comfortable | $500–900k | Solid equity + investments | On track for ASFA comfortable |
| Wealthy | > $1.2M | Multiple properties | Self-funded |

## Key Actions
- Maximise concessional contributions (including carry-forward if eligible).
- Review insurance and estate planning.
- Consider salary sacrifice or after-tax contributions.
- Track education and activity costs.

Official sources: ATO, ASFA, Moneysmart, Services Australia.
