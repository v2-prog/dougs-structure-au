# Ages 50–51: Peak Accumulation & Pre-Retirement Optimisation

**Educational only. Not personal advice. Verify current rules.**

## Life Events
- Peak earning years for many.
- Children may be independent or still at home.
- Focus on maximising super contributions and reducing non-deductible debt.
- Carry-forward unused concessional caps available if total super balance < $500,000.

## Scenarios
### Scenario A: Family of five transition
Children leaving home or still supported. Dual income optimised for contributions.

### Scenario B: Property portfolio + SMSF planning
Multiple properties. Preparing for self-managed super (SMSF) transition. Debt recycling into deductible debt.

### Scenario C: Eco-village / cooperative exploration
Investigating CLT, land trusts, or community living. Using Structure Lab continuity frameworks for governance and succession.

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Large family home vs downsizing preparation.
- Regional acreage for sustainable build.
- Multi-generational support.
- Cooperative or cohousing models.

## Wealth Level Snapshot (couple, end of band)
| Level | Combined Super | Other Assets | Age Pension Path |
|-------|----------------|--------------|------------------|
| Modest | $300–500k | Building equity | Likely partial later |
| Comfortable | $600–1M | Solid equity + investments | On track for ASFA comfortable (~$79k couple) |
| Wealthy | > $1.5M | Multiple properties | Self-funded trajectory |

## Structure Lab Elements
Household Continuity Planner, succession for property/SMSF, cooperative governance.

## Key Actions
- Maximise concessional contributions ($32,500 cap + carry-forward).
- Review insurance and estate planning (testamentary trusts).
- Model retirement against ASFA standards.
- Consider downsizer readiness (age 55+).

Sources: ATO, ASFA June 2026, Services Australia, Moneysmart.
