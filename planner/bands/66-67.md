# Ages 66–67: Retirement Phase Entry & Age Pension

**Educational only. Not personal advice. Verify current rules.**

## Life Events
- Age Pension eligibility begins at 67 (means-tested).
- Full access to super from age 65 regardless of work status.
- Account-based pension minimum drawdowns apply.
- Transfer Balance Cap ($2.1M) limits tax-free retirement phase.

## Scenarios
### Scenario A: Transition complete
Retired or semi-retired. Drawing from super + possible Age Pension.

### Scenario B: Property + SMSF in retirement
SMSF in pension phase. Property outside or inside SMSF. Downsizer already used or available.

### Scenario C: Eco-village / cooperative retirement
Living in community land or cohousing. Structure Lab models for shared assets and succession.

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Downsized home (downsizer contribution already available from 55).
- Multi-generational with adult children.
- Regional or eco-village.
- Rental (higher ASFA cost; more super needed).

## Wealth Level Snapshot (single)
| Level | Super | Other Assets | Age Pension Indicator |
|-------|-------|--------------|-----------------------|
| Modest | < $300k | Limited | Full or high partial |
| Comfortable | $500–800k | Home equity | Partial (ASFA ~$56k single) |
| Wealthy | > $1.5M | Significant | Unlikely / asset-tested |

## Key Actions
- Claim Age Pension if eligible (Services Australia).
- Set sustainable drawdown rates.
- Update binding death benefit nominations.
- Plan for longevity and aged care.

Sources: Services Australia Age Pension, ATO, ASFA, Moneysmart.
