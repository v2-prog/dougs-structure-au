# Ages 14–15: First Work & Independence

**Educational only. Not personal advice. Verify current rules.**

## Life Events
- First casual or part-time jobs (paper round, cafe, retail) where age rules allow.
- Own phone and plan (often parent-paid).
- Growing independence in spending and saving.
- School continues; extracurricular costs rise.

## Scenarios
### Scenario A: Early casual work
Small income, opportunity to open a bank account and track spending.

### Scenario B: Family support focus
Pocket money and chores as primary income. Parents model budgeting.

### Scenario C: Eco / community interest
Early involvement in school gardens, cooperatives or local projects (Structure Lab alignment).

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Living with parents.
- Multi-generational household.
- Regional or rural setting with lower living costs.

## Wealth Level Snapshot
| Level | Savings | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Modest | $0–300 | Pocket money only |
| Comfortable track | $300–1,500 | Consistent saving from work or chores |
| Ahead | > $1,500 | Strong family support or higher casual hours |

## Key Actions
- Open a youth bank account.
- Discuss TFN with parents (ATO).
- Track simple income and expenses.
- Explore Moneysmart resources.

Official sources: Moneysmart, ATO, Fair Work (junior rates).
