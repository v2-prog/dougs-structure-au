# Ages 16–17: Licence, Accounts & Casual Work

**Educational only. Not personal advice. Verify current rules.**

## Life Events
- Learner licence, driving lessons and supervised driving.
- First independent bank account, card and Tax File Number.
- Casual wages at junior pay rates.
- School continues or early transition planning.

## Scenarios
### Scenario A: Learner + casual work
Transport costs begin. Income from retail or hospitality.

### Scenario B: Apprenticeship path starting
Trade pathway interest. Tools and transport costs emerging.

### Scenario C: Family of five support
Older teen contributing to household or supporting younger siblings.

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Living at home (most common).
- Share house or boarding (less common at this age).
- Regional family property.

## Wealth Level Snapshot
| Level | Savings | Super started | Notes |
|-------|---------|---------------|-------|
| Modest | Low | None or minimal | High transport costs |
| Comfortable track | Building | First SG possible | Regular casual work |
| Ahead | Strong | Growing | Family support or higher hours |

## Key Actions
- Apply for TFN (ATO).
- Open bank account and debit card.
- Understand junior pay rates (Fair Work).
- Budget for licence and insurance costs.

Official sources: ATO, Fair Work, Moneysmart, state transport departments.
