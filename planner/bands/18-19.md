# Ages 18–19: Legal Adult, Study & First Work

**Educational only. Not personal advice. Verify current rules.**

## Life Events
- Voting age and full legal capacity.
- Transition from school to university, TAFE, apprenticeship or full-time work.
- HELP (HECS-HELP) debt begins for many.
- Youth Allowance or other income support possible.
- Super guarantee starts with first employer.

## Scenarios
### Scenario A: University pathway
HELP debt, part-time work, Youth Allowance. Focus on budgeting and first super.

### Scenario B: Trade / apprenticeship
Higher early earnings, employer super, possible tools and transport costs.

### Scenario C: Family of five context
Older sibling or parent supporting younger children. Shared household costs.

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Living at home (lower costs).
- Share house.
- Regional campus or on-site trade accommodation.

## Key Actions & Links
- Apply for TFN (ATO).
- Enrol to vote (AEC).
- Check HELP repayment thresholds (ATO).
- Explore Youth Allowance (Services Australia).
