# Ages 30–31: First Child & Household Transition

**Educational only. Not personal advice. Verify current rules.**

## Life Events
- First child for many (around age 31 average).
- Parental leave and temporary dip in household income.
- Move to a larger home possible.
- Childcare costs begin to appear.

## Scenarios
### Scenario A: Family of five emerging
Dual income becomes single or reduced for a period. Government parental leave and family payments relevant.

### Scenario B: Property + planning ahead
Existing property. Considering additional investment or SMSF in future years.

### Scenario C: Eco-village / cooperative interest
Exploring shared living or community models while raising children (Structure Lab continuity).

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Larger family home (mortgage or rent).
- Multi-generational support.
- Regional or acreage for space.
- Cooperative or cohousing models.

## Wealth Level Snapshot (couple)
| Level | Combined Super | Other Assets | Notes |
|-------|----------------|--------------|-------|
| Modest | $100–250k | Building equity | Income dip from parental leave |
| Comfortable | $300–600k | Solid equity | On track for later ASFA |
| Wealthy | > $800k | Multiple properties | Self-funded trajectory |

## Key Actions
- Claim Paid Parental Leave / Family Tax Benefit (Services Australia).
- Review insurance (life, income protection).
- Update will and beneficiaries.
- Model impact of reduced income on super.

Official sources: Services Australia, ATO, Moneysmart, state family services.
