# Ages 20–21: First Car, Housing & Early Career

**Educational only. Not personal advice. Verify current rules.**

## Life Events
- First car (insurance, registration, running costs).
- Share house rent or continued living at home.
- Trade wages or part-time work beside study.
- Super contributions building with regular employment.

## Scenarios
### Scenario A: Share house + study
Rent takes a large share of income. Budgeting critical.

### Scenario B: Trade / full-time work
Higher earnings. Opportunity to start saving for deposit or car.

### Scenario C: Property awareness early
Family discussion of first home buyer schemes or SMSF long-term planning.

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Living at home (lowest cost).
- Share house.
- Regional campus or work accommodation.
- Multi-generational household.

## Wealth Level Snapshot
| Level | Savings | Super | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|-------|
| Modest | Low | Starting | High rent / car costs |
| Comfortable track | Building | Regular SG | Dual income potential later |
| Ahead | Strong | Growing | Family support or higher wages |

## Key Actions
- Review first home buyer schemes (state revenue offices).
- Track rent and car costs.
- Maximise early super (even small amounts compound).
- Consider income protection if full-time.

Official sources: ATO, state revenue offices, Moneysmart, Services Australia.
