Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Buying may only be possible with a partner, a scheme or a lower-priced region. Rent assistance may still apply to some renters.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

A first home with a sizeable mortgage. Repayments take a large share of income, so a buffer is important.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

A larger deposit means lower repayments. Offset accounts, extra repayments and property investment are options.
