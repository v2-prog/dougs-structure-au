Room 7 — Wills & Estate Planning

Brief explanation

A will sets out how you want your assets distributed after you die. Without one, state laws decide. Enduring powers of attorney and guardianship documents let someone you trust make financial or personal decisions if you lose capacity. Superannuation and some jointly owned assets may sit outside the will, so binding nominations matter. These are basic tools for continuity — get professional advice for your situation.
