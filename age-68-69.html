Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Keep careful track of income to stay within pension limits. Travel is often domestic or low-cost.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Pension and super give a steady income. Some money may go to grandchildren and travel.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Investment income and tax planning are important. Gifting rules matter for estate planning.
