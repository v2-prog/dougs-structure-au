Three income scenarios

Income ranges for people aged 65 to 69 who lodged a 2022–23 tax return. Lower is the bottom 10%, middle is the 40th to 60th percentile and higher is the top 10%. Figures are taxable income a year, in 2022–23 dollars, and show where you sit in this age group. Tax-free super payments from age 60 are not counted in taxable income, so real spending power can be higher than the figures shown.

Bottom 10%

Lower income

Up to about $4,600 a year

Keep careful track of income to stay within pension limits. Travel is often domestic or low cost.

If this is you

  • Tell Services Australia about changes in income or assets.
  • Check bills and cost-of-living concessions each year.

Professionals to consider: Services Australia Financial Information Service officer, financial counsellor, a seniors legal or rights service in your state.

40th to 60th percentile

Middle income

$28,000 to $47,500 a year

Income can come from a part pension, super and small investments. Keeping within the thresholds affects the pension.

If this is you

  • Review how gifts and investments affect pension payments.
  • Plan big costs, such as home repairs, in advance.

Professionals to consider: Licensed financial adviser (retirement income), registered tax agent, estate planning lawyer, My Aged Care assessor for future care.

Top 10%

Higher income

Above about $121,000 a year

Retirement income from super, investments and property. Tax on investment income and keeping the estate in order are the main issues.

If this is you

  • Check franking credits, capital gains tax and the transfer balance cap.
  • Update wills and enduring powers of attorney.

Professionals to consider: Licensed financial adviser (retirement and aged care), accountant, elder law or estate planning lawyer.

Source: ATO Taxation statistics 2022–23, Individuals Table 3 (all individuals who lodged a return, men and women combined). Percentiles are interpolated within the ATO income ranges. Taxable income is after deductions and before tax. People who did not lodge a return are not counted. Examples only, not advice. Check advisers on ASIC's Financial Advisers Register and tax agents on the Tax Practitioners Board register.
