Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Less money overall, but costs fall as children leave. Planning for retirement is still possible with consistent contributions.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Spare cash grows. Extra super and mortgage payments make the biggest difference.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Investment property, super and shares all grow. Estate planning gets more detailed.
