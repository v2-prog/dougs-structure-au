Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

The aim is to stay in work as long as possible and to avoid new debt. Check Age Pension rules early.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Selling a larger home could free equity and fund retirement. Costs and tax need checking first.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Complex asset structures need professional advice. Estate planning and tax are the priorities.
