Room 11 — Community Land Trusts & Community Wealth

Brief explanation

A Community Land Trust (CLT) separates ownership of the land from the buildings on it so that land stays affordable for the community over the long term. Other community wealth models include co-ops, mutuals and purpose-driven charities with asset locks. These approaches aim to keep benefits circulating locally rather than extracting them. They are not substitutes for personal financial foundations.
