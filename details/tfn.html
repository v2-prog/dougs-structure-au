Tax File Number & First Bank Account

Most people apply for a TFN and open their first independent bank account around ages 16–18. This unlocks casual work, super contributions and later tax returns.

Key actions

  • Apply for TFN online via myGov or ATO.
  • Open a fee-free youth or everyday account.
  • Give your TFN to your employer so tax is withheld correctly.
