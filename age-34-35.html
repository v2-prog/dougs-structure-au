Five economic considerations

Childcare costs

Childcare costs peak in these years. The Child Care Subsidy offsets part, depending on income.

SourceServices Australia

Return to work

Often part-time at first, which affects super and pay progression.

SourceFair Work Ombudsman

Family car

A bigger or second car adds loan, insurance and running costs.

SourceMoneySmart (ASIC)

Mortgage repayments

Usually the single largest regular expense.

SourceMoneySmart (ASIC)

Super check

Check balances, consolidate accounts and look at fees.

SourceAustralian Taxation Office

Forms and files to look for

  • Child Care Subsidy claim (Services Australia)
  • Family Tax Benefit claim (Services Australia)
  • Super account consolidation (ATO)

Three income scenarios

Income ranges for people aged 35 to 39 who lodged a 2022–23 tax return. Lower is the bottom 10%, middle is the 40th to 60th percentile and higher is the top 10%. Figures are taxable income a year, in 2022–23 dollars, and show where you sit in this age group. The ATO publishes ranges of about five years, so this page uses the range that includes age 35.

Bottom 10%

Lower income

Up to about $17,000 a year

Childcare can cost nearly as much as one wage, so work days and the subsidy need careful planning. Rent or mortgage stress is common.

If this is you

  • Compare Child Care Subsidy outcomes for different working days.
  • Ask a financial counsellor about hardship arrangements with lenders and utilities.

Professionals to consider: Financial counsellor (free, through the National Debt Helpline), Services Australia Financial Information Service officer, community legal centre.

40th to 60th percentile

Middle income

$58,000 to $83,500 a year

Childcare, school costs and mortgage repayments compete with super top-ups and savings. A second income or a full return to work often changes the picture.

If this is you

  • Check that super was paid during any leave.
  • Ask the lender to review the rate if the market has moved.

Professionals to consider: Mortgage broker, registered tax agent, licensed financial adviser (fee for service), insurance broker, estate planning lawyer for a will.

Top 10%

Higher income

Above about $157,000 a year

Higher income helps absorb childcare and mortgage costs. Extra repayments, investing and planning for school fees become practical.

If this is you

  • Discuss an investment loan with an adviser before borrowing.
  • Use the before-tax super cap, including unused amounts from earlier years if your balance allows.

Professionals to consider: Licensed financial adviser (CFP), accountant (CA or CPA), tax specialist, estate planning lawyer.

Source: ATO Taxation statistics 2022–23, Individuals Table 3 (all individuals who lodged a return, men and women combined). Percentiles are interpolated within the ATO income ranges. Taxable income is after deductions and before tax. People who did not lodge a return are not counted. Examples only, not advice. Check advisers on ASIC's Financial Advisers Register and tax agents on the Tax Practitioners Board register.
