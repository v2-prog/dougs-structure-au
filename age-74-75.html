Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Government-funded home support may be available. Assessment through My Aged Care is the first step.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Some costs are met privately, some through government support. Planning ahead reduces stress.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Private care, home modifications and professional support are typically funded privately.
