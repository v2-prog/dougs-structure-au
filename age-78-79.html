Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Subsidised care covers most costs, but waiting times can be long. Early assessment helps.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

A mix of government support and private payment. Means-testing shapes cost.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Higher contributions apply, and private options are common. Professional advice is valuable.
