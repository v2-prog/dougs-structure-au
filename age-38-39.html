Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

School costs are the main squeeze. Support such as school-card schemes and subsidised activities may be available.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

A steady position: mortgage reducing, savings growing, insurance reviewed. Some money goes to the next goal.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Investments outside super are growing. Family trusts or company structures may be discussed with an adviser.
