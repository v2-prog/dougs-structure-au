Room 10 — Grants, Subsidies & Funding

Brief explanation

Governments and other bodies offer grants, subsidies and concessional loans for study (HECS-HELP), housing, energy efficiency, small business, farming and community projects. DGR (Deductible Gift Recipient) status can make donations tax-deductible for charities. Always verify current eligibility, deadlines and conditions on the official website. Scams targeting grants are common — never pay upfront for “guaranteed” funding.
