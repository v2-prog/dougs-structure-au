Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Super balances may be modest, so even small top-ups matter. Age Pension is a possible future safety net.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Larger super contributions, mortgage reduction and help for children share the budget.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Super caps, tax and estate planning shape strategy. Advice from a licensed adviser can be worth the cost.
