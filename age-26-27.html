Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Wages cover living costs but a deposit feels far away. Government support schemes and a shared purchase are options to explore.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Regular saving into a deposit and super. A partner's income can make a purchase plausible within a few years.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

The deposit may arrive early, or family help may shorten the wait. Investment choices matter more than saving rate.
