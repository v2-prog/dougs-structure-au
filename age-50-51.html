Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Super may need a catch-up. Keeping working years going and avoiding debt are strong levers.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

A comfortable retirement may be within reach if contributions continue. Helping children needs to be weighed against it.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

A strong asset base. Tax-effective strategies, and passing on wealth, are now a main focus.
