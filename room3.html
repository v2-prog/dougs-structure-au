Room 3 — Work, Wages & Tax

Brief explanation

Your payslip shows gross pay, tax withheld, super contributions and net pay. You need a Tax File Number (TFN) so the correct amount of tax is taken out. Understanding tax brackets, the tax-free threshold and claiming the right deductions (where eligible) helps you keep more of what you earn. Always check your payslip and keep records.
