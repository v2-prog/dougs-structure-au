Three income scenarios

Illustrative gross incomes: lower about $50,000, middle about $100,000, higher about $200,000 a year. For ages under 18, read these as household incomes. For retirement ages, read them as lower, middle and higher income and savings. They are examples, not official brackets.

~ $50,000 a year

Lower income

Contract terms in retirement villages should be reviewed carefully. Free advice services are available.

~ $100,000 a year

Middle income

Choices between staying put and moving depend on cost, health and family.

~ $200,000 a year

Higher income

Retirement living options are broad. Legal and financial advice protects the estate.
